# About

`AUTHOR // DARKCYBE`

Darkcybe is the author and publisher of this archive. Its focus is practical cybersecurity: incident response, digital forensics, detection, investigation and the engineering that supports them.

Darkcybe 3.0 is a fresh edition. Earlier material is a source to revisit selectively, with its usefulness and technical currency assessed before it returns. The archive is organised around the work a reader needs to do.

## The operations centre

The Death Star / Holonet terminal is the visual setting: spare panels, technical labels and a little Imperial restraint. The content remains plain-spoken. A reader should never need to understand the fiction to understand the method.

## K-2AI // HOLONET EDITORIAL SYSTEM

K-2AI assists with research, structure, drafting and technical review. Occasional labelled callouts make that assistance visible. Darkcybe retains authorship, editorial judgment and the publication decision; AI output is not published autonomously.

Assistance is not evidence of testing. Claims about lab work, field experience or reproduced results must be supported by the corresponding evidence and attributed accurately.

## Corrections and uncertainty

Technical articles should identify their sources, validation basis and last verification date. Corrections that change the meaning of a result should be visible to the reader. Missing evidence remains a limitation rather than an invitation to fill the gap with confidence.
