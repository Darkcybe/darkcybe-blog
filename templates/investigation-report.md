## TL;DR / executive summary

Summarise supported conclusions and unresolved questions.

## Why this matters and scope

State affected behaviour, evidence window and analytical purpose. Attribute public reporting; never imply participation in an incident without supplied evidence.

## Environment and methodology

Identify relevant versions, evidence sources, collection limitations and analysis steps. Identify any lab reproduction separately from the reported incident.

## What happened: timeline

Record events, source timestamps, time zones and evidence links. Separate reported, observed and inferred events.

## Evidence and interpretation

Tie conclusions to artifacts. Explain competing explanations, confidence limits and missing evidence. Include ATT&CK relationships only when they clarify a supported behaviour, with current citations.

## Investigation and detection opportunities

Identify concrete pivots, telemetry and testable detection ideas. Mark untested ideas explicitly.

## Limitations and defender takeaways

State what cannot be concluded, then actionable lessons within the evidence scope.

## References

Link primary reporting and technical sources directly. Public evidence must already be deliberately selected and sanitised.
