## TL;DR / observation

Describe the bounded point. Attribute whose observation or analysis this is.

## Why this matters

Explain the practical consequence or question raised.

## Context and method

Include dates, environment/versions and how the observation arose when relevant. State whether it is source analysis, supplied experience or a lab observation.

## Evidence and interpretation

Give the supporting public evidence or citations, and separate it from inference or opinion.

## Limitations and open questions

## Takeaway

Offer one useful next action or question without turning the note into a general rule.
